About

A developer's eye, a designer's patience.

I started out teaching and managing academic centres before moving fully into software — which is probably why I care so much about how something is explained, not just how it's built. That instinct carried straight into code: I like interfaces that don't need a manual.

Today I split my time between custom frontend builds — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, ASP.NET — and WordPress, where I design, build and maintain sites for real clients: theme customisation, plugin setup, performance and hosting fixes included.
